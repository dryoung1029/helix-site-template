Group strength classes are the heart of Helix. They're structured strength workouts designed to build confidence and lasting results — 60 minutes that blend strength training and conditioning into a full-body session led by an experienced coach.

## What to expect

- **A written plan, every day.** Classes follow a progressive program so you get stronger over months, not just tired for an hour.
- **Coaching at every station.** Small class sizes mean personal attention on your form.
- **Scaled to you.** Brand new or seasoned, the workout meets you where you are.
- **People who cheer for you.** You're never doing it alone. This is a community that pushes, supports, and celebrates each other.

## Who it's for

Adults of every fitness level who want to get stronger in a supportive room — whether you're brand new or experienced.

## How to start

Your first class is free. Book it, show up a few minutes early, meet your coach, and jump in.
