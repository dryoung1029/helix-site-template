New to strength training? Start here. Strong Foundations is a 6-week introductory course that covers the fundamentals: safe lifting technique, how to feel the right muscles working, how to warm up, and how to recover. It runs in a small group with certified coaches who refine your form every week.

- People new to exercise or strength training
- Anyone who wants to refresh their lifting form
- Current Helix members who want to sharpen technique or explore lift variations

Helix regulars, new lifters, and curious humans are all welcome.
