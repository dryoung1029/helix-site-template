Personal training at Helix is intentional, strength-based coaching built for real life and long-term, pain-free performance. Whether you're new to training, rebuilding strength, navigating a life transition, or just want smart, sustainable progress, sessions are designed around your body, your goals, and your season of life.

## What a session looks like

- **A plan with a purpose.** Programming that blends functional strength, mobility, conditioning, and skill work — nothing random.
- **Coaching, not counting reps.** Your coach watches how you move, adjusts on the spot, and explains why.
- **A movement assessment first.** Before we load anything, we look at how you move and talk through your history.
- **Practical nutrition guidance** that supports strength, resilience, and long-term health.

## Who it's for

People who want individual attention, people coming back from time off or an injury, people training for something specific, and people who simply learn better one-on-one.
