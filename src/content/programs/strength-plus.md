Strength+ is a focused training program for people over 50 who want to stay strong, capable, and confident in everyday life. Sessions center on safe, progressive strength work, mobility, balance, and functional movement — whether you're brand new, coming back after a break, or already an experienced lifter.

## What to expect

- **Progressive strength work** that builds the kind of strength you use every day: getting up from the floor, carrying groceries, climbing stairs without a second thought.
- **Balance and mobility** built into every session.
- **Modifications on the spot.** Coaches adjust every movement to fit you.
- **A room full of people like you.** Encouragement, laughter, and no one watching the clock.

## What members tell us

Better strength and mobility. Better balance and confidence moving. More energy. And the thing that matters most over a lifetime: consistency, because they actually enjoy coming.
